# Friends <sub>with</sub> Commits

**The people who keep open source running.**

*Friends with Commits* is a podcast about the people who keep open source running, now that agents can write the code. Hosted by a maintainer and a developer who uses what he builds, we talk to the people behind the repos about why they build, what keeps them going, and what has changed for them in the last year.

No slides, no sales pitch. Just friends with commits.

## Hosts

- **[Johannes Wachter](https://github.com/wachterjohannes)**, the maintainer. Core developer of Sulu, Symfony AI core team, author of Symfony Mate.
- **Alexander Bürner**, the practitioner. Uses open source tools in client projects and hosts [devslove.it](https://devslove.it).

## The question every guest gets

> What has changed in your project in the last year, and what has stayed the same?
